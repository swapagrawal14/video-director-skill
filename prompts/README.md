# Prompt ideas: what to ask an agent once it has this repo

Give your agent the repo link first:

> Use the video-director skill from https://github.com/swapagrawal14/video-director-skill and follow its SKILL.md.

Then ask for anything. The engines are starting points, so mix, stretch and invent. Each prompt below names the engine the agent will most likely reach for.

## Tutorials and how-tos (`screencast/`)
- "Make a 30 s Hindi reel showing how to download a Wikipedia article as a PDF. Record the real site, zoom to every click, add a talking host and pop-up stickers."
- "Record how to create a GitHub repo from scratch and turn it into a 45 s vertical tutorial with STEP chips and sound effects. Blur my email wherever it appears."
- "Show how to use Google Maps offline, Hinglish voice-over, whip cuts between steps, end on a FOLLOW card."
- "Make a 60 s tutorial of my own web app at <url>: log in with a demo account, show the three main features, captions in English."

## Explainers (`handdrawn/`, `motion2d/`, `template/`)
- "Whiteboard explainer, 40 s, on why the sky is blue. Marker draw-on, a talking host, karaoke captions in Hindi."
- "Paper cut-out motion graphics explaining compound interest with a piggy bank that grows each year."
- "Claymation-style short on how vaccines train the immune system. Kid-friendly, 45 s."
- "3D explainer of how a root canal is done, step by step, with labels and exact values listed for me to verify."
- "Desi illustration style: how UPI moves money from your phone to a shop, 30 s, upbeat music."

## Stories and characters (`vidliboom/`, painted hosts)
- "Turn this script.md into a two-character cartoon episode, vertical, with voices."
- "Make a 20 s horror short: a phone screen lights up at 3 am. Slow build, one scare, sound design."
- "Create a new painted mascot for my channel (a chai cup with a moustache), give it five poses and use it to host a 30 s intro."
- "A cute 1-minute bedtime story about a lost star, painted style, soft narration."

## Promos and social
- "30 s product promo for my app: hook in the first second, three feature beats, end card with the download link."
- "Kinetic typography reel of this quote, bold condensed type, beat-synced cuts."
- "Turn my GitHub README into a 20 s launch teaser with screen captures and a banner image."
- "Data story: animate India's internet users from 2010 to 2025 as a bar race, with sources on screen."

## Education and science
- "Explain photosynthesis in 45 s for class 8, Hindi voice, quiz question at the end."
- "Animated diagram of how a four-stroke engine works, slow-motion cutaways, labels on each stroke."
- "Dental hygiene tutorial: correct brushing technique, 60 s, show the angle and timing on screen."

## Remix and stretch the engines
- "Put a real screen recording inside a hand-drawn explainer: whiteboard intro, screencast middle, painted host outro."
- "Make the screencast engine look like a retro VHS tape: new background kind, scanlines, tape-hiss SFX."
- "Build a new engine for lyric videos: words bounce on the beat of a music track I give you."
- "Same script, three styles: whiteboard, claymation and screencast. Render all three so I can compare."
- "Make a series template: one host, one look, so I can produce a new episode every week by changing only the script."

## Tips for better results
- Say the **length**, **aspect** (9:16 reel, 16:9 YouTube), **language** and **voice** you want.
- Name a **reference** (a video or a style) and what you like about it.
- Ask for **stills first** to approve the look before the full render.
- For facts (medical, legal, technical), ask the agent to **list every value for you to verify**.
- Use only material you own or are allowed to use; ask the agent to blur anything personal.

## Cartoons in any art style (`motion2d/cartoon.py`, playbook `docs/CARTOON.md`)
No animation knowledge needed: say the idea and a look (a style name or a screenshot), the agent asks the rest.
- "Make a cute 20 s cartoon: a sleepy girl turns off the lamp and her clothes on the hook look like ghosts, then the light comes back. Pastel style, no voice."
- "Here's a screenshot of a cartoon style I love. Make a 30 s short in that look about a cat stealing my chai. Don't copy their characters."
- "A 6 s seamless loop of a boy riding a bicycle around a tiny planet, flat motion-design style, for Instagram."
- "1930s rubber-hose style cartoon of a toothbrush and a cavity having a boxing match, 30 s, bouncy music."
- "Anime-style emotional 40 s short: a student waiting for exam results at night, rain on the window."
- "Chibi sticker mascot for my channel: make it wave, laugh and facepalm, then a 10 s intro with it."
- "Dark cartoon horror, adult and properly scary: a power cut in an old house, something stands in the corridor. 45 s, 9:16."
- "Crayon kid-drawing style bedtime story about a lost star, 1 minute, soft Hindi narration."
- "Desi gouache style 3-shot episode: a Diwali evening at home, mummy, papa and a naughty kid, hard cuts between shots."
- "Ek chhota cartoon banao: power cut hota hai, kamre mein sab dark ho jata hai aur almirah ke kapde bhoot jaise lagte hain. Cute pastel style, 20 second, voice nahi chahiye."
- "Mere is reference video jaisa style le lo (sirf look, characters nahi) aur ek 30 sec reel banao jisme ek dentist ka chashma gum ho jata hai. Hinglish voice."
